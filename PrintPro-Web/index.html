How a bill is worked out

The same four steps every time. Scroll through test case 1: a student printing 2 black and white pages and laminating 1 sheet.

Asha, Student

Subtotal₹29.00
Discount (10%)−₹2.90
GST (18%)+₹4.70
Total₹30.80
Step 1 of 4

Add up every line

Each service is price × quantity. The program adds the lines recursively: this line plus the total of the rest, until no lines are left.

2 × ₹2 + 1 × ₹25 = ₹29.00
Step 2 of 4

Take off the discount

Students get 10% off and faculty 15%. Each customer type overrides the discount rate; the formula itself is a final method nobody can change.

₹29.00 × 10% = ₹2.90 off → ₹26.10
Step 3 of 4

Add 18% GST on what's left

GST is charged on the discounted amount, not the original subtotal.

₹26.10 × 18% = ₹4.70
Step 4 of 4

Round to two decimals

₹26.10 + ₹4.698 = ₹30.798, shown as ₹30.80. Token 1 is odd, so Asha collects from Tray 1.

token & 1 = 1 → Tray 1
